# Weapon Skill Patch

Adds original-game weapon-granted skills to three T2 chapter weapons and updates the matching armoury card icons. No stat changes, no exe changes.

| Weapon | Added skills | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Angels T2 Plasma Gun (Mastercrafted) | Crippling Shot | T4 "Baal Wrought" Bolt Pistol |
| Black Templars T2 Mastercrafted Power Sword | Riposte, Parry | Riposte from T4 "Axe of Calth" Power Axe; Parry from T1 Power Sword |
| Imperial Fists T2 Mastercrafted Heavy Bolter | Resilient, Killing Blow | Resilient from T4 "Forge Bane" Melta Gun; Killing Blow from T4 "Tempests' Teeth" Chainsword |

The Black Templar sword already had Counterattack and the Parry buff; this patch adds the distinct Riposte ability and makes Parry visible on the card. The Imperial Fist heavy bolter drops Parry in the final revision and uses Resilient plus Killing Blow instead.

The installer replaces six uassets in full (variable-length ArrayProperty edits), backs up each file, and supports rollback. Unchecked by default.
